Y Ventures Group Ltd is an SGX-listed group headquartered in Singapore, made up of five businesses across data, AI and services.

Businesses

  • YV Enterprise

    The group’s flagship e-commerce business. A leading Amazon operator with more than a decade of experience, it helps brands launch, grow and protect their marketplace position through analytics, sourcing and account management.

    About YV Enterprise

  • YV Lab

    The group’s venture and technology studio. It builds and runs live platforms, and brings the same operating approach to product, AI and growth work.

    About YV Lab

  • YV Logistics

    The group’s logistics arm. First built to serve YV Enterprise, it now runs cross-border freight, Amazon fulfilment preparation and inventory management as a managed service for marketplace sellers.

    About YV Logistics

  • AIP

    The group’s AI learning business. Built around Nalar, it helps students turn their own materials into structured study workflows.

    About AIP

  • Junk To Clear

    A junk removal and disposal service for homes and businesses in Singapore, with instant quotes, transparent pricing and a focus on reuse and recycling.

    About Junk To Clear

How the group operates

Data first

Each business is built around the data it creates, treated as an asset that grows in value over time.

Long-term operators

Success is measured in decades, with a preference for durable businesses over short-term results.

Shared capability

Infrastructure, data and talent are shared across the group, so each business can move faster.
