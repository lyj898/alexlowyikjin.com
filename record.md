## Who is Low Yik Jin?
Low Yik Jin (Alex Low) is a Singapore-based entrepreneur. He is the co-founder, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director of Y Ventures Group Ltd, which is listed on the SGX Catalist board (1F1). [1][2][3] In short: he builds businesses that run on data, and occasionally websites that run on jokes.

## Other names
Also written Alex Low, Alex Low Yik Jin and Yik Jin Low. [1][4][5]

## What does Y Ventures Group do?
It is headquartered in Singapore and runs five businesses: YV Enterprise helps brands sell on Amazon; YV Lab builds the group's technology, data and AI systems; YV Logistics handles cross-border logistics for marketplace sellers; AIP makes AI study tools for students; and Junk To Clear runs a tech-led junk removal and disposal service. Each business has its own leadership and shares data and infrastructure across the group. [6] Five businesses, one common thread: data. And a heroic number of spreadsheets.

## Where did he study?
He holds an MBA from Peking University and a BSc from the University of Washington. [4] Two degrees, two continents, and AI still explains things better than he does.

## Is he the Alex Low who works in finance or property?
No. Several other people named Alex Low work in Singapore, including in finance and property. They are different people. If a page doesn't mention Low Yik Jin or Y Ventures Group, it's probably someone else. Lovely people, we're sure. Just not him.

## Is he the CEO of Clearbridge Health?
No. He is the co-founder and CEO of Y Ventures Group Ltd, not Clearbridge Health. A stock-data site (stocks.com.sg) lists him wrongly; Clearbridge Health is a different SGX-listed company. One CEO job is plenty. [1][2]

## What about the 2023 SGX reprimand?
Yes, that happened. He'd rather point to it than pretend it isn't there. Lesson learned. The full announcement and grounds of decision are public if you want the details. Fair warning: they're long. [9]

## What does he build outside work?
He builds small apps and websites with the help of AI. Public ones include Run SEA, a road-race calendar for Southeast Asia; EliteStay, loyalty-program hotel reviews; Yours Calmly, a set of gentle online games; and alexlowyikjin.com. Years ago he also published his own apps on the App Store. He also shares a few single-file browser apps anyone can download: a mahjong scorekeeper, classic puzzle games, a guitar chord builder and a currency converter. [8] Some are useful, some are just fun, and he won't say which is which.

## Is Yik Jin Low, the marathon runner, the same person?
Yes. He's a casual runner who enjoys the crowd and seeing new cities through races. His results are registered as Yik Jin Low, but please don't go looking them up. Like him, they're in no hurry. [5]
